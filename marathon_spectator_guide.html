London Marathon spectator guide for friends supporting Jose on April 26 2026

Spectator game plan

London Marathon / Sunday 26 April 2026

Cheer points
1

Mile 9 early race

Very close to Canada Water Station. It gets very busy, but there's a corner there that offers a good view of the runners.

2

Mile 11 approaching Tower Bridge

Very close to you, very far from us. Less busy. After this, you're free to go eat, explore, or rest until the final stretch.

3

Just after Blackfriars underpass mile ~24

Recommended last cheer point. Runners emerge from the underpass here.

Nearest station: London Blackfriars (rail)

PICK ONE: Blackfriars OR finish

If you cheer at Blackfriars (mile 24), getting to the finish area at Horse Guards in time is extremely difficult. Road closures and crowds make it nearly impossible to move fast enough. We tried spectating last year and couldn't make it. Choose the one that matters more to you.

4

Finish -- Horse Guards only if skipping Blackfriars

Post-race meeting points are alphabetically signposted along Horse Guards Road/Parade. Our letter is TBD (we'll share once we have it from race pack).

After the race

Group photo: CHEEEZ Studio

Korean-style self-portrait photo booth at 130 Charing Cross Rd, WC2H 0LA (near Leicester Square / Tottenham Court Road). Walk-ins welcome, no booking needed. Let's get a group photo to remember the day.

Food: Honest Burgers

We suggest Honest Burgers. Several locations nearby -- pick one and let us know. We will not be making decisions or moving a finger after 26.2 miles.

Covent Garden 33 Southampton St Warren St 117 Tottenham Court Rd Leicester Sq 17 Garrick St

You're also welcome to suggest somewhere else entirely -- just plan it and tell us where to show up.

How to track us

Before race day

  • Download the TCS London Marathon app (here)
  • Add our bib numbers (we'll share these once confirmed)
  • iPhone users: make sure we're sharing locations via Find My
  • You already have our rough pace targets
Gap between cheer point 2 (mile 11) and cheer point 3 (mile 24) is roughly 2+ hours. Plenty of time to grab food, rest, or explore. The London Marathon route map is available on the official TCS London Marathon app and at londonmarathonevents.co.uk.
