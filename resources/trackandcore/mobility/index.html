Track & Core Mobility

A few minutes before and after each run keep you training. Choose the exercises that you like: you do not need all of them every time.

Every easy run

  1. Glutes. One or two exercises, 8 reps each.
  2. Lunge matrix. About 2 minutes, 5 reps in each direction.
  3. Start easy. The first 5–10 minutes are gentle.
  4. Run. Use the effort on your plan card.
  5. Ease off. The last 5–10 minutes are very easy. Then walk for 1–2 minutes.
  6. Myrtl routine. About 5 minutes, two or three times a week.

Stop if it hurts. If the lunge matrix or the Myrtl routine hurts your knees or hamstrings, stop and speak to a coach.

Before a speed session, long run, or race

  1. AIS stretches. You can do these from cold.
  2. Lunge matrix, 5 reps in each direction.
  3. Easy running, 15–20 minutes.
  4. Four to six strides of 20 seconds, at 5 km effort.

Stop if it hurts!! If pain is above a 2/10, stop and speak to a coach.

After a speed session, long run, or race

  1. Jog for 10–20 minutes, more and more slowly.
  2. Walk for 1–2 minutes.
  3. Do a few basic static stretches, about 8 seconds each.
  4. Myrtl routine. About 5 minutes.
