Track & Core Marathon Plan 2027

Welcome

Your first marathon or your tenth: we are happy that you train with us. We believe that we can do hard things, and that we do them better together.

The plan is simple: easy runs, one long run each week, strength and rest. It goes by effort, not by pace. Start easy, enjoy the training and finish with a smile.

The phases

    The sessions

    Effort is a feeling from 1 to 10: 1 is a walk, 10 is all that you have.

    • Easy effort 5/10

      You can hold a conversation. Slow down or walk if necessary.

    • Steady effort 5–6/10

      Comfortable, at an even pace.

    • Long run effort 6/10

      Time on your feet, and practice for food and kit. Walk breaks are fine.

    • Speed effort up to 8/10

      Draft: captains to confirm

      Thursdays. The T&C coach provides the session. No track in December.

    • Strength

      Session A and session B, for legs that go up and down hills. See Strength.

    • Optional

      Rest, or run a Parkrun. You do not miss anything if you rest.
